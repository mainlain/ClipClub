    How ratings work

    A rating of 1–9 costs that many ✨. The app calculates rewards for registered permanent seeders locally. Unused rating funds carry forward as credit; any remaining reward shortfall is minted automatically. Videos advance without a wallet prompt. End the session or choose Settle session to pay up to 100 ratings in one MegaETH transaction. A 0 skips for free.

    Queued ratings are not paid until the batch transaction is confirmed. They are saved if you leave Rate or close the app. Ratings 1–9 also seed the clip until you leave Rate or end the session.

    Multiplier oddsPER ROLL
    Provably fair on MegaETH

    The contract commits your wager to a future drand quicknet round. Anyone can submit its proof to MegaETH’s verifier. The verified randomness fixes the result; the app cannot choose a new one.

    After the round is published, settle the roll to receive tokens or burn the stake. Pending wagers stay locked until settlement.

    Each multiplier plays a random clip with that rounded average rating. A 0 plays no clip.

    Clips play once. Previously watched clips can appear again.

    The multiplier determines your payout even when no clip with that rating is available.
